SFSR-REMS — SPECIFICATION CONFLICTS AND CLARIFICATIONS
================================================================================

Compiled 2026-07-29 while implementing the documentary requirements module.

Every item below was verified against the source documents and against the
code, and each carries a file:line reference so it can be checked rather than
taken on trust. Nothing here has been "fixed" by guessing — where the sources
disagree, the code follows the reading noted under RESOLUTION, and the
disagreement is recorded here for the adviser or the company to settle.

================================================================================
PART 1 — CONFLICTS BETWEEN SOURCES
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C1. Maximum upload size: 3 MB or 10 MB?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[MS]   p.57 — "PDF, JPG, JPEG, or PNG formats with a maximum file size
       specified by the company". No number given.
[DB]   Steps 7 and 8 — "Maximum File Size: 10 MB", stated twice.
[CODE] SFSR-Shared/src/constants.ts — MAX_UPLOAD_BYTES = 3 MB, with the
       comment "Upload constraints taken from the manuscript's Scope and
       Limitation."

That comment is wrong. The manuscript states no figure, so the 3 MB was never
sourced from it. [DB] is the only document that specifies a size.

IMPACT   A buyer photographing an ID on a modern phone routinely produces a
         4-8 MB JPEG. At 3 MB the file is refused before OCR ever runs, and
         the buyer is told their document is too large with no practical way
         to comply short of finding image-compression software.
RESOLUTION  Not changed unilaterally. Raising an upload cap has cost and abuse
         implications that are the company's call, and the Cloudinary free
         tier has limits of its own. NEEDS A DECISION — see Q1.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C2. When does the unit go On Hold?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[DB]   Reservation Terms 1-2, transcribed verbatim into
       SFSR-Shared/src/legal.ts:39-41 —
         "The reservation application shall be processed only after the
          reservation fee has been verified by the Billing Department."
         "Once payment has been verified, the selected unit shall be placed
          under On Hold status while the reservation is being evaluated."
[SUM]  p.2 — "After saving the reservation, the system automatically changes
       the unit status to On Hold to prevent double selling." Immediately,
       with no mention of payment verification.
[CODE] SFSR-Shared/src/reservations.ts:85 — the unit is set to ON_HOLD inside
       the same transaction that creates the reservation, before any payment
       verification exists.

This is the sharpest conflict in the system, because the code shows the buyer
a rule it does not follow: legal.ts:40 is displayed on the reserve page and
promises that the hold follows payment verification.

IMPACT   Two readings, two very different systems.
         Immediate hold — protects against double-selling, which is the
                          study's headline contribution; but a buyer who never
                          pays can hold a unit indefinitely.
         Hold after fee — matches the signed terms and the real business
                          process; but leaves a window in which two buyers can
                          both submit for the same unit, which is exactly what
                          the study set out to eliminate.
RESOLUTION  Immediate hold retained. The double-selling guard is the
         manuscript's central claim, and [MS] p.21 describes real-time status
         synchronisation "eliminating the possibility of double reservations
         or double sales". Deferring the hold would remove the feature being
         defended. NEEDS A DECISION — see Q2. If the immediate hold stands,
         Term 2 must be reworded: a system that displays terms it violates is
         worse than one with no terms at all.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C3. On approval, does the unit become Reserved or Sold?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[MS]   p.21 — "Once the reservation has been reviewed and officially approved
       by authorized personnel, the property's status automatically changes to
       Sold, permanently removing the unit."
[SUM]  p.3 — "On Hold ↓ Reserved / Sold", offering both without distinguishing.
[CODE] SFSR-Shared/src/reservations.ts:182 — sets UnitStatus.RESERVED.
       UnitStatus.SOLD exists in constants.ts but is never assigned anywhere.

IMPACT   Modest for the portal, since both statuses are excluded from the
         public catalogue. It matters for reporting: a "units sold" figure
         would read zero forever.
RESOLUTION  RESERVED retained. An approved reservation is not a completed
         sale — [MS] p.13 places the sale at the Contract to Sell and the Deed
         of Absolute Sale, several stages later. Marking a unit Sold at
         reservation approval would misstate the company's position. Recommend
         SOLD be set when the Contract to Sell is executed, which is outside
         Phase 1. NEEDS A DECISION — see Q3.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C4. Is "Reservation Form" a document the buyer uploads?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[SUM]  p.2 lists "Reservation Forms" among the documents the buyer uploads, in
       both the online and the walk-in flow.
[DB]   Step 8 lists the documents to be uploaded, and Reservation Form is NOT
       among them: BIR Form No. 1904 / TIN, Proof of Billing, Certificate of
       Employment / Proof of Income, Marriage Certificate (if applicable),
       Special Power of Attorney (if applicable), Other Supporting Documents.
[CODE] constants.ts — RESERVATION_FORM is a DocType and is listed in
       REQUIRED_DOC_TYPES.

The two are reconcilable, and the distinction is meaningful. In [DB] the
reservation form IS the online application (Steps 1-10), so there is no
separate document to upload. In [SUM] the walk-in buyer fills in a paper form
which the employee then scans — so it exists as a file only for walk-ins.

IMPACT   As implemented, an online buyer is asked to upload a "Reservation
         Form" that the company never gave them. There is no download anywhere
         in the portal. The requirement cannot be satisfied.
RESOLUTION  Kept as a DocType, because the walk-in flow genuinely needs one.
         Whether it stays in REQUIRED_DOC_TYPES for online buyers NEEDS A
         DECISION — see Q4. This is the most immediately visible defect in the
         current build.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C5. How many projects, and which unit types?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[DB]   Project dropdown names five: The Legaspi Place, Summit Residences,
       Parkview Heights, Harbor View Residences, Greenfield Gardens.
       Unit Type dropdown names six: Studio, Studio Deluxe, One Bedroom,
       Two Bedroom, Three Bedroom, Penthouse.
[REF]  Reference/reference_UI.jpg shows the same five projects in the carousel.
[Verbal] the developer stated three projects in total.
[CODE] scripts/unitData.ts — one project, The Legaspi Place.
       constants.ts — UNIT_TYPES = Studio, 1BR, 2BR, 3BR (four).
       The floor plan sheets in THE_LEGASPI_PLACE/ show exactly those four
       types, and neither Studio Deluxe nor Penthouse.

IMPACT   No functional defect — the catalogue derives everything from the
         seeded inventory, so it is correct for whatever exists. But three
         sources give three different counts, and UNIT_TYPES drives the
         catalogue's aggregation queries: a type present in the data but
         missing from that array is invisible in the catalogue.
RESOLUTION  The code follows the actual floor plan sheets, which are the only
         source carrying real areas, unit counts and prices. NEEDS A DECISION
         — see Q5 — before the remaining projects are seeded.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C6. Birth Certificate
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[MS]   p.26 and p.36 name Birth Certificate as a document category, and p.21
       uses it as the worked example of Document Type Validation: "if the
       buyer selects Birth Certificate but uploads a Driver's License".
[DB]   Step 8 does not list it.
[CODE] BIRTH_CERTIFICATE is a DocType with a keyword signature, and is not in
       REQUIRED_DOC_TYPES.

RESOLUTION  Retained as a situational category. Harmless, and it is the
         manuscript's own illustration of the feature. No decision needed.


================================================================================
PART 2 — SPECIFIED BUT NOT IMPLEMENTED
================================================================================

Present in the sources, absent from the code. Listed so that none of them is
mistaken for an oversight.

G1. RESERVATION FEE AMOUNT
    [DB] Step 4 has a Reservation Fee field, and Term 1 makes processing
    depend on its verification. The system never states an amount and has no
    field for one. A buyer is asked to upload proof of a payment whose amount,
    payee and channel the portal never tells them. Together with C4 this is
    the other half of the same problem: requirements the buyer cannot possibly
    satisfy from information the portal provides.

G2. PURCHASE PRICE BREAKDOWN
    [DB] Steps 2-3 specify Price per Square Metre, Unit Purchase Price,
    Parking Purchase Price and Total Purchase Price, several of them marked
    "system generated". Unit in types.ts carries a single flat `price`.

G3. PAYMENT TERMS AND SUMMARY
    [DB] Steps 4-5 specify down payment percentage (10-50%), promotional
    discount, down payment installment term (Spot Cash through 36 months),
    financing option (Bank / PAG-IBIG / Cash), and a computed payment summary.
    None of it is modelled.

G4. PARKING SPACE
    [DB] Step 2 specifies optional parking with slot number, type (Regular /
    Premium / Tandem), area and price. Not modelled.

G5. THIRTY-DAY DOCUMENT DEADLINE
    [DB] Term 3 and [SUM] p.4 require documents within 30 calendar days of the
    reservation date, after which the employee cancels subject to management
    approval. The wording is displayed (legal.ts:41) but nothing computes or
    stores a due date, and nothing surfaces an overdue reservation to staff.
    Displayed, not enforced.

G6. RESERVATION REFERENCE FORMAT
    [DB] System Confirmation shows "RES-2026-000001". The code uses the
    Firestore auto-ID, displayed as its first eight characters (for example
    "uyvGY91E"). A sequential human-readable reference needs a counter
    document and a transaction to increment it safely.

G7. BILLING, PAYMENT MONITORING, LOAN MONITORING
    [MS] modules 6, 7 and 8 (p.32) and [SUM] p.4 specify Statement of Account,
    balances, payment history, penalties, association dues, and Bank/PAG-IBIG
    loan tracking. None implemented. Believed out of Phase 1 scope — see Q6.

G8. NOTIFICATIONS
    [MS] p.15 — "receive transaction notifications". [DB] — "You will receive
    a notification once your reservation has been reviewed." No email or SMS
    is sent anywhere; the portal shows status in-app only.

G9. CAPTCHA
    [DB] Registration includes "I am not a robot". Not implemented. Related:
    SETUP.md records that Firestore rules cannot express rate limits and that
    App Check with reCAPTCHA is the standard remedy.


================================================================================
PART 3 — IMPLEMENTED BEYOND THE SPECIFICATION
================================================================================

Added because the specification's own goals require them. Recorded so that the
defence is not surprised by a feature no document mentions.

E1. ID SUBTYPE VALIDATION (Stage 1b)
    [MS] p.21 requires the system to detect "Birth Certificate selected,
    Driver's License uploaded". It says nothing about one ID versus another —
    but DocType.VALID_ID treats every government ID as a single category, so a
    buyer selecting Driver's License could upload a PhilHealth card and pass
    Stage 1. IdType, ID_PATTERNS and Stage 1b close that gap. [DB] Step 8's
    "Choose ID type" control implies the same requirement.

E2. FRONT AND BACK OF THE ID
    No source asks for both sides. Philippine IDs carry material data on the
    reverse — restrictions on a licence, address on a PhilSys card — and staff
    cannot verify what they cannot see. Both sides are captured; the front is
    validated, and the back is checked only for being a genuinely different
    image, because the reverse carries no issuing header to match against.

E3. THE ID IS VALIDATED BEFORE THE UNIT IS HELD
    No source specifies the ordering. OCR runs in the browser, so the ID can
    be read and matched with no upload at all; doing it first means a buyer
    with the wrong document never takes a unit off the market, and a failed
    attempt leaves no orphaned file in storage. Consistent with [DB] Step 8,
    which collects the ID during the application.

E4. BUYER-INITIATED CANCELLATION
    [SUM] p.4 describes cancellation as an employee action subject to
    management approval. Buyers can now withdraw their own reservation while
    it is still pending or under review, which releases the unit. Rationale:
    [DB] Term 5 names "voluntary withdrawal of the reservation application" as
    a legitimate cause, and a unit held by a buyer who has moved on is a unit
    the company cannot sell. Staff cancellation exists alongside it. The
    management-approval gate in [SUM] is NOT implemented — see Q7.

E5. STAGE 2 IS ADVISORY, NOT BLOCKING
    [MS] p.21 says a type mismatch is rejected outright, and the code does
    exactly that. It does not say what happens when the name does not match.
    That is treated as advisory: surfaced to the buyer and to staff, but it
    does not block the reservation, because OCR misreads names routinely — the
    manuscript's own example is CRVZ for CRUZ — and blocking would refuse
    legitimate buyers over photo quality. Consistent with [MS] p.57, which
    insists OCR "should not be viewed as a complete replacement for human
    document verification". See Q8.

E6. SITE VISIT REQUESTS REQUIRE AN ACCOUNT
    [MS] p.15 states that prospective buyers "who intend to request a tripping
    schedule or reserve a unit are required to register", so this follows the
    manuscript. Recorded because an earlier build allowed anonymous requests,
    which made trippingRequests the one collection a stranger could write to.


================================================================================
PART 4 — CLARIFICATIONS NEEDED
================================================================================

Q1  (C1) Maximum upload size — 3 MB as built, or 10 MB as [DB] specifies?
    10 MB is friendlier to phone photos and is the figure the buyer is shown
    in [DB]. Raising it also raises OCR time and storage cost.

Q2  (C2) Does the unit go On Hold on submission, or only after the Billing
    Department verifies the reservation fee? If on submission, Reservation
    Term 2 must be reworded — the portal currently displays a promise the
    system does not keep.

Q3  (C3) Should approval set the unit to Reserved or to Sold? If Sold is
    wanted at approval, the later sale stages in [MS] p.13 need reconciling.

Q4  (C4) Must an ONLINE buyer upload a Reservation Form? If yes, the company
    must supply a downloadable form — there is nothing to download today and
    the requirement cannot be met. If no, it should be dropped from
    REQUIRED_DOC_TYPES and kept for walk-ins only.

Q5  (C5) Three projects or five? [DB] and the reference design both name five;
    the developer said three. And are Studio Deluxe and Penthouse real unit
    types, or artefacts of a generic dropdown? The Legaspi Place sheets show
    neither.

Q6  (G7) Are Billing, Payment Monitoring and Loan Monitoring in Phase 1, or
    deferred? They are substantial modules, and the answer changes the
    remaining schedule considerably.

Q7  (E4) Should staff cancellation require management approval, as [SUM] p.4
    states? If so, that is a second approval step and a role that does not
    exist — no MANAGEMENT role is defined anywhere.

Q8  (E5) Confirm that a Stage 2 name mismatch should warn rather than block.
    The current behaviour lets a reservation proceed with a flagged document
    for staff to judge, which matches the manuscript's stance on human
    verification but is not stated explicitly anywhere.

Q9  (G1) What is the reservation fee, and how is it paid? Without this the
    Proof of Reservation Payment requirement cannot be met from information
    the portal provides.

Q10 (G5) Should the 30-day deadline be enforced by the system — a stored due
    date and an overdue queue for staff — or remain a term the company applies
    manually?


================================================================================
NOTE ON ONE FACTUAL ERROR IN THE CODE
================================================================================

SFSR-Shared/src/constants.ts attributes MAX_UPLOAD_BYTES to "the manuscript's
Scope and Limitation". The manuscript specifies no size. Whatever is decided
in Q1, that comment should cite [DB] or the company rather than the manuscript
— a comment that misattributes its source is worse than no comment, because it
stops the next reader from checking.
